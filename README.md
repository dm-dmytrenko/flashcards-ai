## What is this project?
Studying for an exam, learning a new language, or picking up a complex subject usually means wasting hours manually making flashcards. Flashcards AI solves this pain completely: you write down what you want to study, and it gives you everything you need right away. Just review your cards until you've mastered them and they're gone.

## How does it work?

1. **Type and Generate**: Simply type in any topic or subject you want to learn, and AI instantly builds a custom study deck for you.
2. **Review and Clear**: Flip through your cards one by one, test your knowledge, and clear them out as you learn.
3. **Keep Your Progress Private**: Log into your personal, secure account so your study decks and history stay private and saved just for you.
4. **Smart Spaced Repetition**: The app tracks how well you know each card, scheduling reviews intelligently to help you remember things long-term.
